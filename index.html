about the disk

a year, whispered

In November 2002, Razor 1911 released a small calendar of music: twelve FastTracker II modules by blz, each one paired with a month, and four painterly season screens by mime. It checked the month on your computer and opened on the right song.

This browser edition keeps the music, the images and that little seasonal trick. The XM modules play directly, not as pre-rendered audio.
